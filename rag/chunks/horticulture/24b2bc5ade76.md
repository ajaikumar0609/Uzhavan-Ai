# Source: https://agritech.tnau.ac.in/agriculture/glossary/j.html **June budding** A season of budding exists between late may and early June in the northern hemisphere and November to December in the southern hemisphere. **June drop** The final post-bloom shedding of fruits, often occur in late May or June. **Juvenile phase** The phase of vegetative growth of a plant before which it cannot be induced to produce flowers. This period may vary from plant to plant, e.g., rice plants have juvenile phase of one month.
