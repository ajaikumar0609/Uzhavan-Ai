*Methylobacterium* is still not fully known and their vast distribution in natural habitat raises questions of whether the species are habitat specific or they play similar ecological role in different environments. To address these questions, a better understanding of the distribution of *Methylobacterium* strains between and within different ecosystems and analysis of *Methylobacterium* community compositions and dynamics in natural habitats are necessary.
