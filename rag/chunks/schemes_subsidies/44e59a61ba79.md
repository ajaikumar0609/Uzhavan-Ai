In order to encourage the small growers form Tea Producers’ Society (TPS) Tea Board under the 10th plan period has given special subsidies for setting up of TPS as detailed below; **Rate of subsidy for the Tea producers societies (Self Help Groups):** 1. 50% subsidy for purchase of vehicle for transporting green leaves from fields to factory. 2. 100% subsidy for setting up of leaf collection sheds with a ceiling limit of Rs.30,000/- 3. Actual cost will be reimbursed for purchase of Plastic crates, leaf carry bags and weighing machines 4. 25% subsidy for purchase of Pruning Machines subject to ceiling limit of Rs.5,000/- per machine for one Self Help Groups Hence during the period it was proposed to convert all the 20 Quality Tea Procurement and Service centres into Tea Producers’ Societies and Awareness campaigns were conducted to motivate the SHGs to form TPS.
