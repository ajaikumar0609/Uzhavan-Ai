State Plan Scheme --- **Details of Works** - 20 Nos. of Poly house tunnel type Solar Chilli drier @ Rs.2.00 Lakhs will be installed and handed over to the identified Chilli growing farmers group in the 5 major Chilli growing districts (4 nos. in each district) *viz*., Ramnad, Thoothukudi, .Sivaganga, Virudhunagar and Tirunelveli at 100% subsidy assistance to the group --- **Eligibility** -
