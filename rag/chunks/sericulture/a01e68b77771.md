# Source: https://agritech.tnau.ac.in/sericulture/seri_videos and cd.html | | | Sericulture Technologies | | | | VIDEOS AND COMPACT DISC ON SERICULTURE --------------- -- ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------- **1. SERICULTURE IN INDIA** This thirty minute documentation is a window on Indian Sericulture/Silk Industry. It explains various commercially known Indian Silk varieties, and also the governmental strategy ‑both Central and State to develop this industry, besides highlighting the research and development efforts of Central Silk Board. **Available Languages**: English and Hindi **2. PACKAGE FOR PROSPERITY** This video film is devoted to various research innovations of the Central Silk Board relating to mulberry Cultivation and silkworm rearing. It narrates the package of practices developed for mulberry cultivation, both under irrigated and rainfed conditions, and also the improved techniques of silkworm rearing, besides demonstrating the various methods for controlling pests and diseases of mulberry as well as silkworm. **Available Languages**: English and Kannada **3. A STITCH IN TIME** Silkworm disease is one major stumbling blacken the path of successful cocoon crop harvests. Truly therefore, as the title suggests this video film dwells upon the various pathological problems of silkworm diseases to help the rearer re‑orient the silkworm rearing to achieve bumper cocoon harvests. **Available Languages**: English and Kannada **4. BIVOLTINE FOR HIGHER YIELD; MORE INCOME** Bivoltine silk is the trump card for the success of Indian Silk Industry. But, being temperate in nature its rearing under tropical conditions of India calls for its effective scientific management. This video deals at length the various aspects of bivoltine silkworm rearing, diseases and control measures and the economic viability of bivoltine rearing. **Available Languages**: English and Kannada **5. REELING FOR QUALITY SILK** Reeling is the weakest link in the Chain of sericultural activities, and hence needs uncompromising attention , especially in the context of improving the quality of Indian Silk to make it acceptable in the International market. This video is first of its kind and can prove to be the "Bible" of post cocoon technology. **Available Languages**: English, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Marati, Malayalam, Gujarathi, Oriya, Kashmiri and Urdu. **6. CAPSULE FILMS ON POST COCOON TECHNOLOGY** The Central silk Board has produced video capsules on the following post cocoon activities namely Reeling for quality silk, Sorting and Grading, Multiend Reeling, Reeling by products and Water for silk reeling. **Available Languages**: English, Hindi, Kannada, Gujarathi, Kashmiri, Malayalam, Marathi, Oriya, Tamil, Telugu & Urdu **7. STRONG TIES; LOOSE THREADS: WOMEN IN SILK** This video is all about how Indian women are intricately woven in to the warps and welfts of a whole gament of sericulture activities from soil to silk. This film narrates the role of women in mulberry cultivation, silkworm rearing, silk reeling and silk weaving. This video also depicts the economic viability of sericulture as a remunerative avocation. **Available Languages**: English and Kannada **8. PREVENTION IS BETTER THAN CURE** Like any other industrial activity, silk industry is also beset with professional hazards. This video touches upon the health hazards posed by the reeling and grainage activities and precaution measures to be adopted to insulate the workers against such health hazards. **Available Languages**:
