MULBERRY CULTIVATION AND FARM MANAGEMENT** [PART I & II] This film explains visually the different technologies to be adopted in Mulberry Cultivation and farm Management Techniques. THE STORY OF TASAR** This film is about social rehabilitation of nomadic tribes inhabiting the jungles of Madhya pradseh, Bihar, Orissa, West Bengal & Andhra Pradesh through Tasar Silkworm Rearing. Tasar is the second largest variety of Indian silk. This film courses through the ups & downs of the tasar silk industry in India. **Available Language**: English **12. ERI SILK IN THE NORTH EAST** North East India is the jewel of the Himalayas, blessed not only with matchless flora and fauna but also the rich heritage of eri silk. This non domesticated silk is produced in the states of Assam, Meghalaya, Nagaland and Arunachal Pradesh. This video is a composite picturisation of the various facets of eri silk industry. **Available Language**: English **13. GOLDEN MUGA : THE CULTURAL HERITAGE OF ASSAM** The golden muga silk is the exclusive preseve of Assam. No wonder therefore at the rest of India is ignorant about its cultural practices. This video has been adjudged as the best industrial film in the 35th National Film Festival, New Delhi. This film depicts the various facets of muga silk industry from food plant to muga silk fabric. **Available Language** : English **14.
