Satellite Bands Summary
🛰 Sentinel-2 (S2) — Optical
12 spectral bands (443–2190 nm) • 10m resolution • ~56 obs/farm
Bands: B1–B12 + AOT, WVP, SCL, MSK_CLDPRB
Image size: 34×33 px (10m bands) • Cloud-affected during monsoon
📡 Sentinel-1 (S1) — SAR Radar
2 polarizations (VV, VH) + incidence angle • 10m resolution • ~15 obs/farm
Image size: 33×33 px • All-weather (penetrates clouds) • 6-day revisit
🌡 Landsat-8 (L8) — Optical + Thermal
19 bands including thermal (ST_B10) • 30m resolution • ~21 obs/farm
Image size: 11×11 px • 16-day revisit • Only source with LST data
