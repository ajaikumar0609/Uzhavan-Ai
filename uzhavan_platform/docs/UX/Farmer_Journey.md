# Farmer Journey — UZHAVAN AI

## Stage 1: Before the App
Murugan wakes at 5 AM. Checks the sky, calls his neighbor, decides irrigation by memory. No real-time weather, no official pest thresholds, no personalized advice. Bad decisions cost yield.

## Stage 2: Discovery
KVK extension worker demo or neighbor WhatsApp forward. He sees someone get a cited Tamil answer in seconds.

## Stage 3: Onboarding (FP-001)
| Step | Screen | Action |
|------|--------|--------|
| 1 | PhoneInput | Enter mobile number, request OTP |
| 2 | OTPVerify | Enter 6-digit OTP within 5 min |
| 3 | ProfileSetup | Name, district, primary crop |
| 4 | FarmSetup | Area (acres), soil type, irrigation type |
| 5 | Home | Dashboard shows district weather |

**Aha moment:** Home screen shows Thanjavur weather + Tamil prompt: "உங்கள் நெல் பயிரைப் பற்றி கேளுங்கள்"

## Stage 4: First Question (FP-002)
Murugan types: "நாளைக்கு தண்ணீர் விடலாமா?"
Gets: weather + TNAU irrigation guideline + source citation in ≤ 8 seconds.
Trust signal: TNAU URL visible in source card — he recognizes the government institution.

## Stage 5: Regular Use Patterns
| Morning (5–7 AM) | Afternoon (12–2 PM) | Evening (6–8 PM) |
|-----------------|--------------------|--------------------|
| Weather → irrigation decision | Disease photo if leaves look odd | Scheme eligibility check |
| Crop status check | Treatment advice in Tamil | Next week's plan |

## Stage 6: Trust Building
After 3–4 weeks following recommendations: yield holding, decisions faster, less phone calls to neighbors for advice.

## Stage 7: Advocacy
Murugan shows the app to Rajan. Word-of-mouth is the primary growth channel.
